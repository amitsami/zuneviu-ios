# Zunévio discovery artwork

Sixteen mood/genre images generated specifically for this update. Cropped from one AI-generated photographic contact sheet, then encoded as WebP. No external artwork endpoint, logo or artist likeness is used for these banners. Category text is native Compose text, not baked into imagery. App visuals are not a licence to remove the upstream source-code copyright notices retained elsewhere.

## 1.9-rc1 music mark

The approved monochrome, single-beam paired-note artwork replaces the earlier Z mark. Editable master: `branding/zunevio-music-mark.svg`; native vector/adaptive and density-specific launcher assets are in `app/src/main/res/`. Full Home branding uses native Text. Legacy 192px regular/round and 48px previews were visually inspected; compiled adaptive geometry and black background are separately checked. These are asset checks, not screenshots of the running RC1 app.
